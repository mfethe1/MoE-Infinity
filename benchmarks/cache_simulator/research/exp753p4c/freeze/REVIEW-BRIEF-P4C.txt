## Context
exp753 (sealed) had exactly one parity failure class: prompt-4 rows on engine cells had n_new=21
because the MoE-Infinity native generate IGNORES min_new_tokens (it stops at EOS). The frozen
gate (operative contract) requires: engine p4 passes iff (n_new==64 AND len(out_ids)==64 AND
out_ids[:21]==engine_ref[4]) where engine_ref[4] is a 21-ID stream ENDING in EOS 50279.
exp753p4c re-runs the engine cells with ONE change: suppress the engine's EOS stop-break so the
decode runs exactly 64 steps with EOS kept in-stream at index 20 ("step-past-EOS"), then judges
the rows with the UNCHANGED frozen analyzer.
