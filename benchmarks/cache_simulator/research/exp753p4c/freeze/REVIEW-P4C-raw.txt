### Findings

1. **P0 — The operative prereg is not in front of the reviewer.** Q5's live questions (probe/full window definitions, timing denominators, which metrics are frozen vs. exploratory, rerun disposition) live in PREREG-P4C.md §3/§4, which is not quoted. 2. **P1 — Warmup and stat baselines are also forced; the "ONE behavioral change" has a second-order footprint.** The flip block executes *before* `gen()`/warmup, so the warmup generation decodes 64 forced steps instead of stopping at EOS. Consequences: offload-cache residency at `stats_start` (s0), `policy_routed_hits/misses` deltas, and GPU thermal/clock state at the first measured row all differ from sealed exp753. Fine if the frozen gate is parity-only; if any frozen metric consumes cache stats, per-row latency, or probe/full timing windows, p4 rows now carry 3× the decode steps and must be re-baselined or explicitly excluded in the prereg before freeze. The analyzer judges only `out_ids`/`n_new` — it will not protect you here.

3. **P1 — HF-vs-engine asymmetry at index ≥ 20 is structural, not cosmetic.** HF's `min_new_tokens=64` suppresses EOS by *logits masking*; the engine suppresses nothing. So `hf_ref[4]` does not contain EOS at index 20 — its token at 20 and its entire 64-token suffix are a different generation regime (EoS-masked continuation) from the engine's (true EOS kept at 20, then unmasked post-EOS garbage). Any cross-arm agreement/quality/latency-keyed-to-token metric over the p4 stream is invalid at indices ≥ 20, and the engine p4 suffix must be quarantined from any downstream consumer (it is not meaningful output). The frozen predicate already encodes this (engine p4 judged on 21-ID prefix only) — the prereg must state it so no secondary metric silently violates it.

4. **P1 — "The exact build under test" is not pinned anywhere.** The smoke/measurement run against `PYTHONPATH=/home/mfeth/exp753/src`, a mutable tree. Record per-run sha256 of `moe_infinity/engine/generation_loop.py`, `moe_infinity/entrypoints/big_modeling.py`, plus `refs/engine_ref.json`, `prompts.json`, and `analyze753.py` in the run manifest. 5. **P1 — Coupling proof (`model._native_generation_engine`) covers one construction path only.** The flip is a private-attribute mutation done once at startup. If the engine is ever (a) constructed lazily per `generate()`, (b) rebuilt between rows, or (c) a different class per cell config (gpu-route off vs. on, different policies/ratios), the startup readback still says `eos_after=-1` while rows silently run unforced. That fails *closed* (p4 shows `n_new=21` → gate fail) rather than corrupting results, but it burns the frozen run. `res["force_steps"]["row_lens"]` is recorded post hoc and never asserted. Fixes: (i) fail fast inside the runner — abort if any forced engine row yields `n_new != 64`; (ii) extend the smoke to at least one cell per distinct engine construction path (minimum: `--gpu-route off`) and one `--timing on` invocation, which the smoke never exercises.

### Verdicts on Q1–Q5

- **Q1: Yes.** With `eos_token_id = -1`, both stop-checks are unreachable (sampled vocab ids are ≥ 0), the `range(sp.max_tokens - 1)` loop runs to exhaustion → `1 + (max_tokens−1) = max_tokens = 64` tokens, EOS stays at its sampled index, and `_sample` is pure greedy argmax with no masking. Conditions: `max_tokens == 64` (from `--max-new 64`), the elided loop body has no other break, and `generate()` doesn't trim at EOS — the latter two are empirically confirmed by smoke S1 (64 tokens, EOS at 20) but should be hash-pinned (findings 4, 9).
- **Q2: Yes, with caveats.** Without the flag the diff is behaviorally inert (arg parsing, `force_steps=None`, one extra res key). With the flag the only semantic change is the attribute flip (plus prints and the `row_lens` recording). Caveats: warmup runs forced too (finding 2), and analyzer-safety of the top-level key is inferred from a row-level predicate (finding 7). Flag-inertness for all non-p4 rows is provable from sealed evidence: only p4 emitted EOS before step 63, so no other row's stream can change under the flip.
- **Q3: Yes, satisfiable.** The break fires only *after* EOS is appended at index 20, so tokens 0–20 are computed identically forced/unforced; `out_ids[:21] == engine_ref[4]` (including EOS at 20) is achievable, and `n_new == len == 64` holds by construction. Smoke S1 satisfied the full predicate. Conditional on prefix determinism (finding 6).
- **Q4: Sound in structure, leaky in details.** The mechanism + inertness + S2 mutation contrast (metric demonstrably bites: 21 vs 64) is the right design. Defects are the ones in finding 10 — chiefly single-config coverage and unasserted quiet gate.
- **Q5: Covered above.** The material hidden confounds are: forced warmup altering cache/stat baselines (2), the HF/engine post-EOS regime mismatch (3), and the fact that no timing/probe-full metric can be signed off while PREREG-P4C.md's denominators remain unseen (1). No P0 defect exists in the mechanism or the diff itself.

**Bottom line:** the mechanism is correct and the diff is minimal and fail-closed; the gate is satisfiable and was empirically satisfied. Block launch only on finding 1 (attach/pin the frozen prereg + analyzer + build hashes); findings 2–6 must be explicitly closed or scoped out in the prereg before freeze.
