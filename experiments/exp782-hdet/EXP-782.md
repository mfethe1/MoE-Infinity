## Question (PREREG-782)

does decode output determinism differ between host-callback mode (H, MOEI_EXP752_MODE unset,
mode=0) and event-poll mode (RC, =RC, mode=2)? Prior: exp752 H_1 diverged (foreign GPU, load
21.5); exp758 HB1 diverged / P34 stable; exp760 RC 0/10 CP ub 0.259 — H arm never powered.

## Interpretation
The H instabilities seen in exp752/exp758 did NOT reproduce under standardized co-tenancy in
5 fresh H cells; and attempt-1's single CGH1 divergence did not recur in the re-run. H-side
divergence exists in the wild but is intermittent and condition-dependent — under this
protocol neither arm diverged. Claim limit: "no divergence observed in n cells under these
manipulations" — never "deterministic". For PR #353/#354 this removes the concern that RC
(event-poll) is any LESS stable than H (host-callback) under controlled co-tenancy.
