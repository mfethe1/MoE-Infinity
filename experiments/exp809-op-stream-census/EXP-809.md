# EXP-809 — Op-stream census: 3,008 aten dispatches per decode token, 97% overhead-class

**Question.** exp808 showed the decode burn is distributed per-op orchestration (top symbol 0.4–0.6%; python3.12 44.7% of userspace PCs, torch dispatcher ~25%). How many aten ops execute per decode token, and which op classes dominate? (This quantifies the per-op orchestration tax directly.)

**Method.** `TorchDispatchMode` counter wrapping generation (`__torch_dispatch__` increments a `Counter`), OLMoE-1B-7B-0125-Instruct on RTX 6000 Ada (CUDA 1, offload r05, lcnu, 4 threads, greedy). Arms: WARM_8 throwaway, then BASE_B_128 and BASE_B2_128 (8 prompts × 128 tokens, `probe8.json`). ops/token = (full_total − pre_total)/n_tok with a 1-token pre-phase subtracted to exclude prefill. Counting mechanism smoke-verified on CPU before launch (attempt-4).

**Findings.**
1. **The decode op stream is 3,008 dispatches/token, exactly replicated across cells** — the per-op tax is deterministic and structural, not noise.
2. **97.3% of ops are overhead-class** (detach, views, copies, allocations, broadcasts). Real GEMM-class (mm/addmm/bmm) is 81.6/tok = 2.7% — consistent with 16 layers × ~5 GEMMs; everything else is glue.
3. **`detach` alone is 1,191/token = 39.6% of the entire stream** — defensive tensor-boundary churn, plausibly kv-cache/expert-glue related, is the single largest addressable op-class target.
4. **Quantitative closure with exp807/808**: 3,007.9 ops × 24.0 µs/op = 72.1 ms/tok — precisely the measured main-thread userspace burn (exp807 utime 72.1 ms/tok). The interpreter+dispatch overhead story is now measured end-to-end: no hot function, but ~3k × 24 µs of per-op tax across interpreter, dispatcher and allocator.
5. Layer-structure fingerprints (pow/mean/rsqrt at 65.5/tok = 4.1/layer) confirm the counter resolves per-layer norm glue exactly as expected — sanity check on the measurement itself.

**Implication.** Any op-fusion / dispatch-batching / graph-capture attack that halves overhead-class ops would recover ~36 ms/tok of the 72 ms burn; eliminating redundant detaches alone bounds ~28 ms/tok. This is the concrete target exp808 pointed at.
