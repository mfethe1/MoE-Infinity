## Question

## Interpretation
The one shipped engine feature that attacks per-access dispatch latency head-on makes
decode ~1/3 slower on consumer sm_120 hardware. Config surface survey (same day):
`MOE_GROUPED_DECODE` is hardware-gated to RTX PRO 6000 (env can only disable it);
`gpu_only_expert_routing` (sync-free dispatch) is already default-on in our baselines;
`num_threads=16` probe regressed −56% (oversubscription). With speculative prefetch
falling back to eager Python routing (conflicts with the native sync-free path), the
conclusion is: **the config-level dispatch surface is exhausted — every knob is either
gated off, already on, or a regression. Breaking the dispatch wall requires
native/kernel-level batching work (port grouped-decode kernels beyond pro6000, or a
Python-free prefetch path).**
