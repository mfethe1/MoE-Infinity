## Mechanics note (honest)

sampler807.py crashed at tick 1: reading `/proc/<pid>/task/<tid>/syscall` returns
**EPERM** for a sibling process under Yama ptrace_scope=1 (stat/wchan are
world-readable; syscall is ptrace-gated). All census JSONLs are empty (verified 0
bytes). Probe-side `/proc/self` reads are unaffected — the primary measurement never
depended on the sampler. Repair extractor `extract807b.py` (sha16 4d25055d): P1 and
S2 formulas unchanged from preregistration; G1's census constant (0.30) applied to
the authoritative st_share instead of the (unavailable) census share, corroborated by
exp805's frozen R-share; S1 recorded as FAIL(census-unavailable) — no repair claimed.
Lesson: `/proc/<tid>/syscall` is not a non-ptrace observation channel on
ptrace_scope=1 systems; future censuses must run as an ancestor or use prctl
PR_SET_PTRACER (which is exactly what exp808's sampler will do).
