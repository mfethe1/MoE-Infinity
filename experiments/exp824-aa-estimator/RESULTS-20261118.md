## Finding: token-level determinism floors do not transfer — they are noise

1. **Process-locality confirmed at n=6.** Six fresh processes at the identical frozen
   config produced g1 spanning 0.4719–0.5667 (range 0.0948), none within 12.86% of the
   0.6503 floor imported from exp821 (which itself superseded exp820's 0.5143 at the same
   config).
2. **The deeper result — within-pair A/A token agreement.** Identical arms (A1 vs A2)
   run back-to-back in the same process agree at token level only:
   - per-battery pair-mean: B1 0.4780, B2 0.5316, B3 0.4933, B4 0.4941, B5 0.4689, B6 0.5233
   - per-pair range across all 36 pairs: 0.3340–0.6152
   Token lists are 8 prompts × 128 tokens per block (n_tok 1024).
   Greedy decoding under this engine is approximately coin-flip nondeterministic at token
   level even within a single process.
3. **Consequence.** The exp820/exp821 "floors" were single-process samples of a noisy
   distribution, not lower bounds. Any prereg gate on absolute token-agreement rate
   measures process noise, not determinism. Block-to-block agreement vs the first measured
   block within a process spans 0.3428–1.0 (B3 min to reference max) — even the
   within-process instrument has no stable floor.

## What survives, what changes

- **Survives:** paired ABAB wall-clock comparison within a battery (un-instrumented
  walls, per-block Σdecode-wall/Σactual-tokens). The A1=A2 null structure is sound; the
  estimator itself was simply never reached.
- **Changes:** future preregs must NOT gate on an imported token-agreement floor.
  Determinism instrumentation becomes *reported* (non-gating) context. The A/A estimator
  validation must be re-run with agreement reported and G1 removed (or replaced by a
  battery-local self-calibration), which is a new frozen attempt, not an amendment.
