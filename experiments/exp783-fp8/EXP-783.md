## Why no speed move — the finding
Residency accounting: B 52,443 hits / 753 misses (98.6% resident), F 53,120 / 312 (99.4%).
At ratio 0.75 the expert set is ~99% resident; decode wall = fixed per-access dispatch/
handoff overhead (fires on every one of ~52k routed accesses), which is bytes-independent.
Halving expert bytes (resident 3.28→1.96 GB, store 13.84→7.41 GB) cannot move a wall made
of round-trips. This REFUTES the exp781 inference "16.6 GB/s ≈ bus ceiling ⇒ bytes-bound":
same tok/s at half the bytes ⇒ pipeline is latency/dispatch-bound at high residency, not
bandwidth-saturated.

## Where FP8 DOES pay (today, measured)
- Resident bytes ×0.60 (3.28→1.96 GB): at fixed VRAM budget, ~1.7× more experts resident
  ⇒ fewer misses at smaller device_memory_ratio (RAM-utilization lever, the program's goal).
- Store size ×0.54 on disk; model load 38–44 s → 24 s (×0.55–0.65).
