## Verdict
**The U-shape did not reproduce.** Within this battery the right arm is refuted: 0.65 was *faster* than 0.50 under both policies (penalties −2.2 lcnu, −1.2 s3fifo — i.e., the "penalty" premise of G2 is invalid here). Only the left arm reproduces: 0.35 is 7.9%/13.5% slower than 0.50 (lcnu/s3fifo). The exp819 optimum-0.50 claim (receipt #136) is not a reliable effect.

Cross-battery caveat (exp820 noise lesson): lcnu@0.65 has measured 89.9 (exp819), 82.3 (exp820), 82.3 (exp821); lcnu@0.50 79.9 (exp819) vs 84.5 (exp821). Battery-level drift exceeds the curve's right-arm effect, so only within-battery statements are claims — and the two within-battery sweeps disagree on the curve's shape. Ratio tuning at [0.35, 0.65] is below reliable resolution; the only robust capacity statement is "0.35 is too small."

S1/S2 failures are localized to S65 (agreement 0.4666; rep 125.3 outlier): treat the S65 cell as contaminated, not as evidence of output divergence.
