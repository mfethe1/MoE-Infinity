# EXP-815 — Faithful fused-RMSNorm: reduction order, not weight ordering, causes the shift — no output-preserving fusion at token-identity bar

**Question.** exp814's fused RMSNorm won −13.5 ms/tok but shifted outputs materially (agreement 0.542 vs 0.749 self-floor). Which change caused it: (a) fp32 weight-mul ordering (kernel multiplies weight in fp32; HF multiplies in input dtype after cast) or (b) the kernel's internal reduction order? If (a), a faithful variant is output-preserving and upstreamable.

**Method.** 6-arm A/B, identical shape to exp814: WARM + HOOK_B/HOOK_B2 (hook-patched engine baseline) then FAITH_WARM + FAITH_B/B2. Faithful variant = `torch.rms_norm(x_fp32, shape, None, eps).to(input) * self.weight` — HF's exact operation ordering; **only the kernel's internal reduction order differs**. (API verified live: None-weight maxdiff 1.19e-7 vs manual fp32 math.) First-try clean battery, RESTORE-VERIFIED (542a27f478c99661).

**Diagnosis (the point of the experiment).** With weight-mul ordering matched exactly, agreement still fails (0.539 < 0.5985 floor) — same shape as exp814 (0.542 < 0.744 floor, two independent batteries). **Kernel reduction order is the cause; no output-preserving fused RMSNorm exists at the token-identity bar.** The math is equivalent; the numerics are material; greedy decode amplifies at near-ties (FAITH self-agreement 0.458 — amplification, not corruption).

**Cross-battery caveat (recorded):** the HOOK self-agreement floor drifted between batteries (0.749 exp814 → 0.6035 exp815) — cross-process nondeterminism is itself variable. Both cross-variant agreements (0.542, 0.539) landed below their respective floors: consistent signal despite floor drift.

**Performance ledger (both variants, both replicated):** fp32-weight fusion −13.5 ms/tok & −327.6 ops/tok (exp814); faithful fusion −10.2 ms/tok & −262.1 ops/tok (exp815). Chain: hooks (exp812) −13.5 + best fusion −13.5 ≈ **−27 ms/tok (21%)** available.

**Decision path for owner.** For identity-graded paths (this program's gates): keep HF RMSNorm. For upstream/production (where evals, not token identity, are the acceptance bar): exp814's variant is the better optimization and standard practice. Next optimization targets unaffected by this question: linear reshape 16%, rope 8% (exp813 attributions).
