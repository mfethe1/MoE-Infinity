# EXP-814 — Fused-RMSNorm substitution: −327.6 ops/tok and −13.5 ms/tok more, but numerics shift is material (S1 FAIL)

**Question.** exp813: residual 1,823 ops/tok = HF model math, RMSNorm forward ≈ 39% (65 calls/tok × 5 tracked ops: fp32 upcast, eps-add, scale-mul, downcast, weight-mul). Does replacing OlmoeRMSNorm.forward with the native fused `torch.rms_norm` kernel (torch 2.11) reduce the op stream and wall without divergence?

**Method.** 6 arms on the exp812-hook-patched engine (source patch applied at battery start, RESTORE-VERIFIED after): WARM + HOOK_B/HOOK_B2 (new baseline) then FUSED_WARM + FUSED_B/FUSED_B2. Fusion is script-level monkey-patch: `upcast → torch.rms_norm(x_fp32, weight cached-fp32, eps) → downcast` (3 dispatches/call vs ~8). CounterMode census + walls + full token lists; agreement gate per exp813 method. First-try clean battery.

**Gates (preregistered).** P1 ops/tok drop ≥ 400; G1 wall drop ≥ 5 ms/tok; S1 best HOOK-vs-FUSED agreement ≥ HOOK-vs-HOOK − 0.5pp; S2 HOOK wall replication ≤ 3%.

**Adjudication.**
1. **P1 FAIL-as-written = threshold miscalibration, effect real and exactly predicted net**: original RMSNorm = 8 dispatches/call, fused = 3 → net 5/call × 65 calls/tok = **325 predicted vs 327.6 measured**. The prereg "expect ~520" forgot the fused side retains 3 dispatches. Op evidence: HOOK's `mul` 218k + `add` 133k (RMSNorm eps-add + two muls) collapse to rope-only `mul` 85k; `add.Tensor` leaves the top-8 entirely.
2. **S1 FAIL is a real signal, not noise**: fused runs agree with hook runs at 54.2%, *below* the 74.9% hook self-agreement baseline; even FUSED-vs-FUSED self-agreement drops to 63.1%. Per the prereg numerics note, the fused kernel (fp32 weight-mul ordering + different reduction order) shifts outputs measurably; greedy decoding amplifies at near-ties. Mathematically equivalent, numerically material — the optimization is real but **not output-preserving at this program's bar**.
3. **Chain wall to date (instrumented, within-experiment deltas only): hook fix −13.5 ms/tok (exp812) + fusion −13.5 ms/tok (exp814) ≈ −27 ms/tok combined (~21% of the 127 ms/tok CounterMode baseline).**

**Next decision (owner):** (a) accept fused RMSNorm as standard numerics-equivalent optimization (industry practice; downstream agreement of the *model* should be judged by evals, not token identity), (b) try a faithful variant (`rms_norm` without weight, then `.to(input)*weight` in HF's exact ordering) to isolate whether the shift is weight-ordering or reduction-order, or (c) hold fusion out of any upstreamed change set.
