## The finding — neither miss count nor bytes per miss sets the decode wall
Miss accounting (frozen analyzer, summed policy_routed hits/misses):
- B25: 123,651 misses (46.0%); F25: 63,556 (23.7%) — FP8 halves misses (×0.514).
- B50: 57,442 misses (21.4%); F50: 624 (0.2%) — FP8 eliminates 99% (×0.011).
- Working-set mechanism CONFIRMED (prereg map branch 2): fp8 buys fewer evictions, not
  faster transfers — capacity lever.
- But throughput does not track misses: F50 (0.2% misses) is SLOWER than B50 (21.4%
  misses): 11.22 vs 11.64 tok/s. Eliminating ~57k misses moved the wall the wrong way.
- Bytes-per-miss falsifier (cross-cell, matched miss RATE ~22–24%, not a controlled arm):
  F25 at HALF the bytes per miss is 5.8% slower than B50 (10.96 vs 11.64 tok/s).
- Prereg verdict map: rc3 everywhere ⇒ next lever is batching/pipelining expert reads or
  GPU-side dispatch, NOT byte reduction at any residency. Unlocalized in this design: the
  per-access cost difference between the legacy and uniform_fp8 residency paths (offsets
  >57k eliminated misses at ratio 0.50); no profiling in this run.
