**MOVE.** Ship `device_memory_ratio=0.5` for this envelope (config-only change;
upstream-shippable). Confirms exp790's decomposition: the dispatch stall is
substantially expert-fetch wait — residency caching recovers ~31%. Remaining

batteries baseline at 0.5; (c) handoff-latency attack now has a clean signal.
